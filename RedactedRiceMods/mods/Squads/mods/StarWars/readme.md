## Squads

### Star Wars Rebels
Brought from a long, long time ago in a galaxy far, far away, these Rebel starships are fighting back against the evil vek empire wherever it may be.

This squad has some heavy hitting weapons but can suffer from lack of board control options. The best way for these guys to deal with enemies is to eliminate them directly instead of pushing them around.

#### Luke Skywalker Pilot
Pilot Luke joins the fray. With his force focus ability, he can use his turn to focus with the force
to deal double damage on his next attack.

### Star Wars Empire
The Empire is not about to let the Vek take over without putting up a fight. Join the empire to crush
the Vek just like the Rebels.

This very uniqued squad has high damage, large areas and lots of collatoral damage. The Vek can
destroy the world if you destroy it first... Their unique passive will help reduce the cost of
most destroyed buildings to merely human lives.

This squad also introduces MechFORGE to replace the innate upgrades of the mechs with very thematic
and customized upgrades for the specific mechs in the squad.
